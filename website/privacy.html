Privacy policy

This policy explains how Pirate’s Plunder (“the game”) handles information when you play. It covers advertising provided by Google AdMob and analytics provided by Firebase Analytics.

Information used by the game

The game uses third-party services that may collect information from your device when you use the game. The information can include app and device identifiers, device and app details, approximate location derived from your network connection, gameplay and interaction events, advertising activity, and diagnostic information. What is collected can depend on your device settings, consent choices, and the services’ current configuration.

Google AdMob advertising

Google AdMob may use device identifiers and information about your device, app activity, and ad interactions to deliver, measure, and help protect advertising. Depending on your location and choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. You can manage advertising and privacy settings through your device and any consent choices presented in the game.

Google explains how it uses information from apps that use its services in its Partner site policy and provides additional information in its Privacy Policy.

Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics helps us understand how players use the game, such as which features are used and how sessions progress. It may collect app events, app instance identifiers, device and app information, and related usage data. We use these insights to understand and improve the game. For details about Google’s data practices, see the Firebase privacy and security information.

How information is used

Information from these services is used to operate and improve the game, understand usage, show and measure ads, and help detect misuse or technical problems. We do not use this website to collect account or payment information.

Choices and children

You can review your device’s privacy and advertising settings and make any choices offered in the game. The game is not intended to collect personal information directly from children. Where required, advertising and analytics settings should be configured to follow applicable age and consent requirements.

Third-party services and changes

Google’s services are governed by Google’s own privacy terms. Their practices may change over time. We may update this policy when the game or its services change; the date above indicates when it was last revised.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, visit the support page and use the developer contact option on the Google Play listing.
